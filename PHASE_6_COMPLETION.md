## 🎨 DESIGN CONSISTENCY

### **Typography System**
- **Headers**: Weight 800-900, letter-spacing refined
- **Body**: Weight 500-700, line-height 1.7
- **Labels**: Uppercase, letter-spacing 0.05em
- **Values**: Weight 700 for emphasis

### **Color Semantics**
- **Pass (≥60%)**: Green (#2E6B45)
- **Fail (<60%)**: Red (#C63D2F)
- **Warning**: Yellow/Gold (#D9A441)
- **Neutral**: Muted tones for secondary info

### **Visual Elements**
- **Gradients**: Multi-stop for brand identity
- **Shadows**: Subtle depth system (2px - 12px)
- **Borders**: 1-2px with semantic colors
- **Radius**: 10-14px for modern feel
- **Spacing**: Consistent 20-40px rhythm
